Coach Swift

Privacy policy

Svenska

1. Who is responsible

Coach Swift is made by Emanuel Moberg, Sweden, who is the controller of personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: coachswiftapp@gmail.com.

Coach Swift has no accounts and no servers. Almost everything you log stays on your phone, where only you can reach it. This policy explains the few exceptions.

2. Data kept on your phone

The app keeps your workouts, foods and meals, weight, body measurements and body fat, progress photos, sleep times, plans and settings in its own storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and the developer never receives it.

Some of this is health data. Because it never leaves your phone unless you move it yourself (section 5), no one but you processes it.

3. Barcode lookup: Open Food Facts

When you use Scan barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a non-profit food database based in France (openfoodfacts.org), to look the food up. As with any internet request, their server also sees your IP address. Nothing else about you is sent.

Legal basis: providing the feature you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Their privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

4. Label photos: Google ML Kit

Photo of label reads the nutrition table on your phone with Google's ML Kit. The photo is not sent anywhere and is not kept after it is read.

ML Kit may send Google LLC technical information about how it runs (device type, app and ML Kit version, performance), never your photo or what it read. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a working feature (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Details: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

5. Backups and exports

Back up, Export data and automatic backups write files only to places you choose: a folder on the phone, Google Drive or another service. The developer never receives them. If you pick a folder that a cloud service copies, that service's own privacy policy applies to the copy.

6. Email

If you write to coachswiftapp@gmail.com (also through Send feedback or Support), your address and message are used only to answer you, and deleted when no longer needed for that. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Send error report puts the app's last errors (what went wrong, when, and where in the code) into that email. They are kept only on your phone until you choose to send them, and you can read them before you do.

7. Permissions

Camera: progress photos, barcodes and food labels, only while you use them. Notifications: reminders you turn on, and the sleep alarm. Exact alarms and showing over the lock screen: the sleep alarm. Internet: the barcode lookup. Ads and payments also use the internet, only when you ask for them (section 12).

8. What the app does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reports and no selling or sharing of data. There is no account to create. The one exception: an ad you ask for yourself (section 12).

9. Children

Coach Swift is meant for people aged 16 and over.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), correct (Art. 16) and erase (Art. 17) your personal data, to restrict (Art. 18) or object to (Art. 21) its processing, and to receive it in a portable form (Art. 20).

Since your data is on your phone, you can do most of this yourself: change or delete anything in the app, export it with Export data, or delete it all by uninstalling the app. For anything else, write to coachswiftapp@gmail.com.

You may also complain to a data protection authority; in Sweden that is IMY, the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (imy.se).

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is in the app and What's new says so. This version applies from 8 Nov 2026.

12. Ads and payments

The app shows an ad only when you tap Watch an ad (Settings, Support the app). Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.) then loads it and may use your phone's advertising ID, IP address and device details to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK, Google asks for your consent first. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Details: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Subscriptions and tips are paid through Google Play under Google's terms. The app only learns whether a purchase went through; it never sees your card or account. Legal basis: the purchase you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
